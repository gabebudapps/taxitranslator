Taxi Translator

An iPhone app for showing your driver where to go

Taxi Translator turns a hotel, address or place into a large card in the driver's language, helps you understand what the driver says, and has common taxi phrases ready to show. Driver languages: Cantonese, Mandarin, Taiwan Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese and Indonesian.
