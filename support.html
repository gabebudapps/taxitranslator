Common questions

The name on the card looks wrong

Place names come from Apple Maps and Wikidata, with machine translation as a last resort. Each card shows how sure the app is about the name, and names can still be wrong. Show the driver the map too. If you have the correct local name, for example from your booking or the hotel's card, use Edit name on the confirmation screen.

Which languages and places does it cover?

Cantonese, Mandarin, Taiwan Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese and Indonesian. In other countries the card shows the name in your language and the map. The app's own screens are in English.

Does it work without data?

A saved card's name, address and phone number open without a connection. The map and street photo need one. Searching for a new place also needs a connection.

Translation says it needs a connection

Translation uses Apple's language downloads. Connect to the internet and let iOS download the language, ideally before your trip.

Speech recognition isn't working

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are on for Taxi Translator in iPhone Settings. If it still fails, for example with poor signal, the driver can type instead. Machine translations can be wrong, so check anything important.

How do I delete a saved place?

Touch and hold it and choose Delete. For your hotel, choose Not my hotel first. Saved places are only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

What is free, and what does the purchase add?

You can make 3 driver cards for free, with all phrases and Talk included. A one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription, unlocks unlimited new cards and saved places. Cards you have already made stay available.

I bought Unlimited and it is locked, or I have a new iPhone

Use Restore Purchases on the purchase screen while signed in to the Apple Account you bought it with. It needs a connection.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Reporting a problem

Please include the place you searched for, the driver language, which screen you were on, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you expected to see.
