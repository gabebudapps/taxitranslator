Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

The short version. We collect nothing and we run no servers. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising. To find places and understand speech, the app uses Apple's services and Wikidata, and it sends them only what each request needs. The places you save stay on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive your searches, your location, your saved places, what you or your driver say, or anything else you do in the app. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no tracking and no third-party SDK.

What stays on your iPhone

The places you save, including the one you mark as your hotel, are stored in a database on your iPhone. They are not sent to us. If iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup is on, iOS may include this database in your backup, as it does for other apps.

To delete a saved place, touch and hold it and choose Delete. For your hotel, choose Not my hotel first. Deleting the app removes the database.

Like any internet connection, these requests reveal your IP address to the service that receives them. Apple's and the Wikimedia Foundation's own privacy policies apply to what they receive. We have no access to any of it.

We send the Wikimedia Foundation nothing about you. The app identifies itself to Wikidata by its name, its version and our contact email, as Wikimedia asks, and that information is about the app rather than you.

What the permissions are for

Every permission is optional. If you decline one, the rest of the app keeps working.

  • Location, while using the app. Finds nearby places and shows the items described above. The app does not track your location in the background and does not store it.
  • Camera. Used only on the scan screen, to read an address or place name with Apple's on-device text scanner. The app does not save or send images.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used only after you tap to listen on the Talk screen. The app uses speech recognition on your iPhone where it is available and may otherwise use Apple's speech service, as described above. The app does not save the audio.

Translation of what is said uses Apple's Translation framework, which runs on your iPhone once the language has been downloaded. Reading text aloud also happens on your iPhone.

The QR code

A card's QR code contains a link to the destination's location on a map. It is only used if your driver scans it, and then opens on the driver's own phone in a maps service such as Google Maps or, in mainland China, Amap. That service's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is handled by Apple under your own Apple Account. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether the item has been bought.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we knowingly collect nothing from anyone of any age.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or erase. Your saved places are on your own iPhone and you can delete them there, as described above. If you email us, we keep your message and address only as long as needed to deal with it, and you can ask us to delete it.

If the law of the country where you live gives you data protection or consumer rights you cannot contract out of, you keep them. Otherwise this policy is governed by the law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
